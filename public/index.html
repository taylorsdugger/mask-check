What the levels mean

LowNo mask needed.
Wastewater is low and not rising.
WatchNo mask needed yet, but keep one on you.
Wastewater is low but rising.
ElevatedMask in crowded indoor places this week.
Wastewater is moderate and not rising.
HighMask indoors in public this week.
Wastewater is moderate and rising, or high.

Wastewater level is CDC's scale (very low to very high) for how much virus is in the sewage compared with each plant's own baseline. Rising means CDC's trend estimate says growing or likely growing, or CDC's forecast has state hospital admissions up 20% or more three weeks out. For New York City, NYC Health's ER visits going up two weeks in a row counts too.

Risk combines the CDC wastewater viral activity level (at home, the highest of the four local plants; elsewhere, the level most people are at) with the CDC epidemic trend estimate for the local health service area. It also counts as rising when CDC's ensemble forecast has state hospital admissions up 20% or more three weeks out, and for New York City when NYC Health's ER visits are up two weeks in a row. All of it updates once a week. Mercer County has no plant of its own, so it leans on Rock Island and Davenport.

Sources: CDC wastewater, CDC Rt estimates, CDC ED visits, CDC forecasts, NYC Health. Not medical advice.
